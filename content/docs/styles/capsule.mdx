## Examples

### Badges

`.capsule` is a pill-shaped overflow-hidden container. Put two `.tag` children inside — the container clips their corners and slightly enlarges them — and you get a shields-io style two-color badge in seconds.

<ComponentPreview name="capsule-badge" />

### Composition

The left tag acts as the label, the right one as the value. Colors are plain utilities on each tag. The container itself works on `<span>` or `<a>`.

```html
<span class="capsule">
  <span class="tag bg-neutral-600 text-white">build</span>
  <span class="tag bg-emerald-600 text-white">passing</span>
</span>

<a class="capsule" href="#">
  <span class="tag bg-neutral-600 text-white">docs</span>
  <span class="tag bg-violet-600 text-white">litefy.dev</span>
</a>
```

## Notes

- `.capsule > .tag` neutralizes the tag radius (`rounded-none`) and bumps padding to `px-2.5 py-1` for badge proportions — no manual radius juggling.
- More than two tags work too: the container just clips whatever is inside.
